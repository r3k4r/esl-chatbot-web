How it works
From sign-up to real fluency.

Step 1 of 6

01

Create your account

Sign up with your email or with Google. It’s free.

02

Unlock your AI tutor

Confirm your email with a 6-digit code, or link Google, and the Free plan switches on.

03

Chat with the tutor

Pick a topic or just say hi. It answers at your level and corrects you gently.
