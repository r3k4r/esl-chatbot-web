# Product

## Register

product

## Users
English learners (students) practising with an AI tutor by text and voice, plus their tutors and the admins who run classes and subscriptions. Many learners are beginners to intermediate (A1-B2): they read English slowly, so every screen must be quick to scan and never wordy.

## Product Purpose
Tutelage AI helps learners practise English daily: chat and talk with an AI tutor that corrects them gently, review vocabulary, track goals, and join tutor-led classes. Success is a learner who opens the app, knows what to do in one glance, and practises without friction.

## Brand Personality
Calm, clear, encouraging. A quiet study space, not a game. Orange is the one warm accent; everything else is neutral.

## Anti-references
- Loud SaaS dashboards: gradient hero cards, glowing buttons, big-number metric templates.
- Walls of text in AI replies: tutor answers stay short (30-60 words), with highlighted key words and short bullets.
- Warm beige/cream surfaces that muddy in dark mode.

## Design principles
- Neutral flat surfaces: grey page, near-white cards with a hairline border, no shadows. Dark mode is each layer one clear step lighter.
- Hover is one grey step, selected is two; never a solid orange or red fill for a hover.
- Compact and predictable: same controls, same spacing, screen to screen.
- AI chat behaves like a real AI app: it opens blank, and a session exists only after the first message.
- Minimum 14px for anything a learner reads or clicks.
